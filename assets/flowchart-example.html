Workflow

Document approval as a flowchart

As long as the review asks for changes, the loop of editing and resubmitting keeps running. Only an approval leads to publishing.

Symbols

  • Action · something is done
  • Decision · several exits
  • Loop-back · path returns
  • Authorthe person writing

Flow

Step 1

Author

Create a draft

The author works on a separate copy. The published version stays untouched until the draft is approved.

ConcreteLike a photocopy of a file on your own desk: the original in the cabinet stays as it is until the copy is signed off.

Step 2

Author

Edit the draft

Each change is saved with a short note, so every state can be traced individually.

Step 3

Author

Upload

The draft is uploaded to the shared workspace and becomes visible to others.

Step 4

System

Request review

The request asks to adopt the draft as the official version. Changes, discussion and results come together in one place.

Step 5

Reviewer

Review

A second person reads the changes, comments on individual passages and decides.

  • Approved: continue with step 6.
  • Changes requested: back to step 2. The request stays open, step 4 is skipped. Every further upload updates it automatically.
Approved

Step 6

System

Publish

The draft becomes the official version. If mandatory checks are configured, they must have passed beforehand.

Step 7

System

Remove the draft

The finished draft is deleted. The history stays attached to the request.

Step 8

Author

Refresh your copy

The author pulls the new official version so the next change starts from the current state.

Open

  • Whether a second person reviews in your setup is not known. When working alone, the reviewer role drops out and the request itself serves as the checkpoint.
  • Whether mandatory checks or protection rules exist is not known. The flow shows the standard case, not your setup.
